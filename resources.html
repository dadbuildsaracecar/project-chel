REFERENCE SKETCHES
BRAKE DUCTING — FRONT & REAR
Front: ducting run at minimum from the front opening back to both front rotors — planned and sketched. Rear: ducting from the 1/4 bubble window was considered, but airflow naturally routes around the bubble, so ducting there wouldn't meaningfully help cooling — decided against for cooling purposes, though it'd still look the part and could help keep that airflow from washing into the cabin.

HEAT & NOISE INSULATION MATRIX

Cockpit heat/noise treatment strategy. Chassis and roll cage are steel tube; cockpit "walls" (floor, tunnel, footbox panels, rocker floor panels) are riveted aluminum sheet — thin, point-fastened, and prone to resonance. Roof is the fiberglass body shell, continuously bonded, low resonance risk by comparison.

Radiant heat barrier (DEI Floor & Tunnel Shield II) — blocks heat from soaking into a panel. Apply to the face facing the heat source. Vibration damping (DEI Boom Mat Pro CLD) — kills panel resonance/drumming. Apply to the face you hear from — cabin side.
