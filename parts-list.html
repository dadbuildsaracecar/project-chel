Build / Project Goals
Mission Statement — pg. 1
  • Build a Shelby Daytona replica that can be used for near-daily driving.
  • Not a show car — this car will get dinged, scratched, and damaged. It should look like it's been driven.
  • Made for maximum driving enjoyment — should arouse all the senses. Loud, vibrates, rattles. Won't always be comfortable.
Ford 289, 300–550hp, 7500+ rpm, EFI Tremec TKX 5-speed 2015+ Mustang IRS Mustang brakes — ease of maintenance Air conditioning Manual steering Hydraulic clutch Custom dash / gauge layout Heat insulation / mitigation Lockable bulkhead behind seats Front / rear brake ducting Closable flap for bubble 3/4 vent window Weather sealing Non-blue color scheme — retain racing decals/numbers
"Building this not only for me to enjoy, but to share that enjoyment with as many other people as possible."
